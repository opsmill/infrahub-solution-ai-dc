## Executive Summary

This spec/plan follows `005-sonic-vendor-support`'s pattern closely and, in several places, proactively applies
lessons SONiC only learned mid-implementation: the loopback/vtep-role exclusion from the physical-interface
loop, the `evpn_sessions`/`ipv4_sessions` address-family split, the tenant-overlay FRR block's own
`overlay_asn is not none` guard, and the loud-comment fallback when no addressed `vtep`-role interface exists
are all specified up front (SONiC research.md D13, defects 1–4) rather than left to be rediscovered. There is
no equivalent to SONiC's blocking yamllint finding (E1 in its own critique) — `swp[a-b]` ranges are short,
slash-free bracket expressions, so no generated YAML line approaches the 140-character limit.

Two lower-stakes gaps remain, both about confidence-labelling rather than correctness: the contract asserts a
couple of ifupdown2-specific mechanics (`link-down yes` for admin-down ports; omitting physical-port
membership from `bridge-ports`) with more certainty than research.md's own stated verification standard
("documented convention, to be confirmed by the human reviewer at SC-001, not verified here") actually
supports, and one real Cumulus convention (STP guards on VNI interfaces) is silently absent rather than
explicitly placed out of scope. Neither blocks proceeding — both are cheap documentation fixes, applied below.
